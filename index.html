Key sessions — how to run them
Strides

6–8 × 20 s at ~3:15–3:30/km, flat. Full 60–90 s walk/jog recovery. Relaxed, tall, fast turnover. Coordination drill — never breathless. 2–3×/week.

Short hill sprints

6–10 × 10–12 s near-maximal on 8–12%. Full 2–3 min walk down. Zero lactic intent. If rep 8 is slower than rep 2, you did too many. Ignore HR. 1–2×/week, all block.

Sub-threshold (flat or uphill)

HR 158–166, RPE 7. 2–3 min easy float jog between. Uphill version on 6–12% grade or treadmill at 8–12% — ignore pace entirely. The single most transferable session for the gravine climbs, and the answer to flat Riga.

Descent repeats (Sigulda)

5–8 × 2–4 min controlled downhill at moderate effort. Cues: high cadence, short steps, quiet feet, relaxed shoulders, eyes 4–5 m ahead not at your feet, arms wide, land under your centre of mass, lean with the slope not back, don't brake with an extended leg. Not trying to go fast — training quads to absorb load for hours. 2–3 days of soreness the first time is the adaptation.

Power-hiking

Hands on quads, short quick steps, hips forward, 6:30–8:00/km. Practise every long run. Rule: hike when the grade is steep enough that hiking isn't slower, or when running would push HR over the cap — whichever comes first. Not a fixed gradient trigger.

Back-to-back

Friday long + Saturday 1h45–2h easy. Saturday is deliberately slow. Running economically on damaged legs is the adaptation, and it's the honest substitute for a longer single run. Two all block: 26 Sep, 3 Oct.

If you get sick — the re-entry ladder
Neck check first. Fever, muscle aches, or any below-the-neck symptom = absolute rest. You're 39; myocarditis after viral illness is not theoretical.
The ladder
Day 0 → symptom-free +24 h: zero training. Not "easy" — zero. Walking and the foot routine only. Eat at full training intake minus ~300 kcal. Don't drop into a deficit while ill — that's how the SHBG flag worsens and the second illness follows the first.
Days 1–3 post symptom-free: 30–45 min Z1 (≤130), flat, alternate days if RHR is still >3 bpm over baseline. Gate to advance: RHR within 3 bpm of baseline and HRV within 1 SD, two consecutive mornings.
Days 4–7: daily easy running at 60–70% of pre-illness weekly duration, nothing over 90 min. No strides, no hills, no loaded gym.
Days 8–14: one long run at 60% of pre-illness longest; reintroduce strides. First quality no sooner than 14 days post symptom-free, and it's sub-threshold, never threshold.
Ramp cap: don't exceed the last completed pre-illness week's hours until two consecutive weeks at ≥85% of it are banked.
Plan-level rules — decided now, while healthy
≥10 days lost before 20 Sep: delete nothing. Re-enter on the ladder, drop that week's quality, carry on. There's enough runway.
≥10 days lost 20 Sep – 10 Oct: the block downgrades, it does not compress. Delete in order: one Sigulda visit → remaining sub-threshold work → the sharpener. Protect: one long run per week, and the rehearsal moved to 8–10 days after return. Goal becomes "finish, sub-12". Do not reclaim missed long runs — that's what turns a ten-day cold into a DNF.
≥10 days lost after 10 Oct: you do not race. Not "see how it feels". Pre-committed here, in August — because the decision made on 20 October by a man who has trained ten weeks and paid for flights is not a decision, it's a rationalisation. This is what the cancellation insurance is for.

First thing to check
Did the August 2026 Helsinki panel actually happen? Your file records one scheduled for 17 to 21 Aug. If it exists you have a five-week-old baseline taken post-illness and pre-build, which is the cleanest reading available. If not, draw now.
Panel

SHBG · total and free testosterone · LH and FSH · ferritin · transferrin saturation · full blood count · hs-CRP · zinc.

Draw conditions matter more than the panel. Fasted, 07:00 to 09:00, at least 48 h after a hard session. Testosterone has a strong diurnal rhythm, and an unstandardised draw compared against Nov 2025 is not a measurement.
Watch free testosterone and body mass, not SHBG. SHBG moves on a weeks-to-months timescale and is not a validated monitoring tool in men.
Mid-block panel, early December: ferritin, full blood count, hs-CRP. If ferritin falls under a 70% load increase it falls during the ramp, and this is the only blood test here that could change a decision while the block is still running.

Substitution ladder when footing kills a session

Same session different route → same session shorter on the safest loop → same duration all easy → treadmill same duration → bike or gym same duration → rest day. Never skip to the bottom.

The Sigulda substitute is not equivalent. Stairs and the arena hill give you climbing, but eccentric specificity cannot be replicated on flat ground. If a Sigulda day is cancelled, say so in the check-in.
